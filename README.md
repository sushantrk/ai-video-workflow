# How I Make AI Videos With One Prompt 🤖🎬

This is the full workflow behind my AI presenter Reels. No camera, no studio and no editing skills needed.

---

## 🧰 Tools I Use

| Tool | What I use it for | Link |
|---|---|---|
| ChatGPT | Writing the script and the video prompt | https://chatgpt.com |
| Claude | Writing the script and the video prompt | https://claude.ai |
| Claude Code | Editing, joining clips, captions and automation | https://claude.com/claude-code |
| Google Flow | Turning the prompt into a video with my avatar | https://labs.google/flow |

You only need **one** of ChatGPT or Claude for the writing part.

---

## 🪜 The Workflow (Step by Step)

### Step 1: Decide your topic
Pick one simple idea for your video, for example: *"One AI that can do video editing, photo editing and automation."*

### Step 2: Ask ChatGPT or Claude for the script and the Google Flow prompt
Paste the master prompt below (change the topic), and you will get:
- Strong hooks for the first 2 seconds
- A short talking script
- A ready-to-paste **Google Flow prompt**
- An Instagram caption with hashtags

### Step 3: Install Google Flow and sign in
Download the app on your phone (or open the website) and sign in with your Google account.

### Step 4: Create your own avatar
Set up your avatar in Google Flow. This is the person who will speak in your video. It takes only a few minutes.

### Step 5: Paste the prompt and generate the video
Copy the Google Flow prompt from ChatGPT or Claude, paste it into Google Flow and generate. **One click, and the video is ready.** ✨

If you don't like the result, generate again or change a few words in the prompt.

### Step 6: Edit and finish with Claude Code (optional)
Use Claude Code to:
- Join two or more clips into one Reel
- Upscale the video to 1080x1920 for Instagram
- Make a cover image from the best frame
- Add subtitles or an AI voiceover
- Check the frames for text problems before posting

### Step 7: Post it
Download the final video, add your caption and post it.
On Instagram, turn on the **"AI info"** label for AI-generated videos. ✅

## 📝 Master Prompt (copy and paste)

```
I make short AI videos for Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. Write everything in simple English.

Topic: [WRITE YOUR TOPIC HERE]

Please give me:
1. Three strong hooks for the first 2 seconds
2. A short talking script (10 to 30 seconds) for an AI presenter, simple and energetic
3. A Google Flow prompt for a vertical 9:16 video: a presenter speaking to the camera in a modern studio with warm lights and a microphone, with bold animated text on screen for the key words
4. An Instagram caption with 6 hashtags
```

## ⚡ What Claude Code Can Do For Creators

- 🎬 **Video editing:** cut, crop and trim videos from start to end
- 🔗 **Join clips:** merge many short clips into one Reel
- 🖼️ **Photo editing:** remove backgrounds and resize photos
- 🎙️ **AI voices:** add voiceovers in different languages
- 💬 **Subtitles:** transcribe speech and add timed subtitles
- 🖼️ **Covers:** make a cover image from any frame
- ✍️ **Captions:** write captions, hashtags and DM replies
- ⚙️ **Automation:** run the whole process from zero to the final video with one command

You talk to it in plain English. No coding skills are needed.

---

## 💡 Tips

- Keep each clip short (around 8 to 10 seconds) and join them later.
- Put the main words on screen in **big, bold text**.
- The first 2 seconds decide everything, so use your strongest hook first.
- Always check the video before posting: spelling, text overlaps and lip sync.
- Be honest and label AI-generated content.
